# Vertical Playbooks

Pick the playbook that fits the business, then build the prompt on the master
template (`prompt-template.md`) using this playbook to set the **mood, palette,
CTA, signature sections, animation flavor, data to pull, and safety notes**. Most
businesses fit one cleanly; blend when a business straddles two (e.g. a groomer +
retail shop, or a dealer + repair shop). Match the depth of the worked examples, but
let real specifics — not padding — earn the length.

**Choose the playbook and tier from the business's OWN language, not its name.** The
name is a weak, often misleading prior: a "med spa," "studio," or "wellness center"
can be premium OR trust-tier depending entirely on how the owner presents it. Read
their site copy, IG bio, price points, and reviews and let *their* register decide.
Only fall back to a name-based guess when the footprint is too thin to read — and then
lean conservative and say so.

Two broad tiers cut across verticals:
- **Premium / experience tier** (barbers, salons, restaurants, detailing, boutique
  wellness, luxury e-commerce): dark or richly styled, animation-forward, booking/
  order-driven, brand-voice-heavy, real menu/service/price specifics pulled from
  source. Light on "verification checklist" scaffolding.
- **Trust / service tier** (handyman, trades, lawn, pressure washing, auto repair,
  pet care with health/safety): verification-first, licensed-trade cautions, big
  "do not invent" and checklist blocks, estimate-driven. Still needs human copy.

---

## Barber / Salon / Personal Grooming

- **Mood:** clean, modern, premium, confident, "slightly dark/luxury." Masculine for
  barbers; adjust for salons/spas. Should make people want to book immediately.
- **Palette:** near-black/charcoal background; gold/bronze/beige/metallic or brand
  accent; strong editorial typography.
- **Primary CTA:** "Book Appointment" (wire to the real booking link — Square,
  theCut, Booksy, etc.). Secondary: "View Services," "Call the Shop."
- **Signature sections:** full-screen hero with rating/address badges; About as a
  grooming-destination story; Services/Pricing cards pulled from the real booking
  menu (real names + prices, each with a Book Now button); Gallery (masonry, from
  Instagram); Why Choose Us (4 benefit cards); Instagram "Follow the Cuts" section;
  strong Booking CTA band; Location + hours.
- **Animation flavor:** loading screen with blade/barber-pole/razor-swipe motion;
  animated logo reveal; sticky glass nav; scroll fade-ins; service-card hover; button
  glow. Elegant, not cheesy.
- **Pull:** Instagram handle + photos, booking link + real service menu/prices, Google
  rating/reviews, address, hours, walk-in/same-day/open-7-days facts.
- **Safety:** verify the Instagram account actually matches the location before
  pulling photos; note if the provided IG doesn't match the city.

## Restaurant / Food / Takeout

- **Mood:** custom, local, authentic, craveable — explicitly NOT a generic AI/template
  restaurant site. Food-forward, real photography, editorial menu.
- **Palette:** dark charcoal/brown; warm off-white type; cuisine-appropriate accent
  colors used as section accents, not a rainbow (e.g. sauce colors for wings).
- **Primary CTA:** "Order Online" (wire to the real ordering platform — SpotOn,
  Toast, DoorDash, etc.). Secondary: "View Menu," "Call Now," "Get Directions."
- **Signature sections:** bold food-first hero with real dish photo + badges; a quick-
  order strip of favorites right under the hero; full Menu with real items; a Sauces/
  Specialties/Signature-items section (use the real named items); Combos/Deals;
  Gallery of real food; Reviews; Location + hours; sticky mobile order bar.
- **Animation flavor:** cuisine-themed loading (sauce drip, fryer basket, steam);
  menu-item hover lift; flavor-chip hover; food image reveal on scroll.
- **Pull:** real menu items and prices, signature/named items and flavors, the brand's
  own slogans from their site/IG, ordering + reservation links, Yelp/Google themes.
- **Safety:** don't invent menu items or prices; keep allergen/health claims off unless
  provided.

## Pet Grooming / Pet Care / Boarding / Training

- **Mood:** warm, friendly, trustworthy, clean; premium but approachable. Owners are
  trusting you with a family member — reassurance matters.
- **Palette:** softer, warmer, or playful depending on brand; can still be modern.
- **Primary CTA:** "Book an Appointment" / "Book a Groom." Secondary: "Call,"
  "See Services & Pricing," "View Gallery."
- **Signature sections:** hero with a real pet/groom photo; About (the groomer's story,
  handling style, "same groomer each time"); Services (full groom, bath & brush, breed
  cuts, nail/teeth/ear, de-shedding, add boarding/daycare/training if offered) with
  real prices where found; a "Meet the Groomer/Team" section; Gallery of real befores/
  afters; Reviews; booking/appointment form; policies (vaccination/health) section;
  Location + hours.
- **Animation flavor:** gentle paw/scissor/bubble motifs; soft fade-ins; card hovers.
- **Pull:** services + pricing, booking link, IG/Facebook photos, Google/Yelp reviews,
  breeds served, add-ons.
- **Safety:** flag vaccination/health/behavior policies to confirm; don't promise
  outcomes for training/medical grooming.

## Home & Trade Services (handyman, lawn, pressure washing, cleaning, HVAC-adjacent)

- This is the **trust/service tier** — follow the full worked example
  (`example-worked.md`). Verification-first, licensed-trade disclaimer, big
  verification checklist and "do not invent" list.
- **Primary CTA:** "Request a Free Estimate" (only say *free* if a source lists it,
  still confirm). Secondary: "Call Now," "Text Us," "See Our Work."
- **Signature sections:** the 22-section spine — per-trade service sections, project
  gallery + before/after, how-it-works, service-area map, estimate form, FAQ.
- **Pull:** Google/Yelp/BBB/Thumbtack/Angi/Nextdoor data, state registry, service
  categories from posts, hours, service area.
- **Safety:** heavy. No fabricated licenses/insurance/warranties/free-estimates/
  emergency-24-7 claims; residential-address privacy note.

## Auto (Used-Car Sales / Detailing / Repair)

- **Mood:** trustworthy, honest, family-owned for sales/repair; premium and sharp for
  detailing. Not luxury-mega-dealer.
- **Primary CTA:** sales → "View Inventory"; detailing → "Book a Detail"; repair →
  "Schedule Service." Secondary: "Call," "Get Pre-Qualified," "Value Your Trade."
- **Signature sections:** sales → inventory grid with filters + vehicle-detail
  component, financing (compliant, no guaranteed approval), trade-in, on-site service,
  why-buy-here; detailing → package tiers, add-ons, before/after gallery; repair →
  services, book, reviews.
- **Animation flavor:** road/route line, subtle reveals, sticky call/inventory bar.
- **Pull:** inventory feed/photos, dealer license #, hours, financing/payment info,
  Google/Yelp/Cars.com/BBB, state registry.
- **Safety (sales):** no guaranteed credit approval, APRs, warranty/AS-IS, or vehicle-
  history claims unless confirmed; use `AutoDealer` schema.

## E-commerce / B2B Wholesale

- **Mood:** matches the product — luxury/global for commodity B2B, clean/retail for DTC.
  If the client gives a reference image, match its look closely.
- **Primary CTA:** "Shop" / "Shop Wholesale" / "Request Quote."
- **Signature sections + build:** product catalog, product detail pages, cart, checkout,
  order confirmation, order-tracking page with tracking-number field, optional account
  flow, and an editable product data structure so the client can add/edit products
  later. Everything future-editable with reusable components.
- **Animation flavor:** cart drawer slide-in, checkout step transitions, tracking-
  status animation, tasteful brand-appropriate reveals.
- **Pull:** product list, pricing/MOQs, brand/reference image direction, categories.
- **Safety:** payment integration as connect-your-own-credentials (Stripe/etc.); never
  hardcode secrets; note where the backend connects.

## Multi-Location (subdomains)

- Use when one brand wants several location sub-sites under one domain (e.g. an ice
  cream brand with 5 locations, each its own subdomain).
- **Build:** one parent brand site + one sub-site per location; shared components; a
  per-location data file (address, hours, phone, reviews, map, menu/inventory, photos,
  schema). Specify the subdomain architecture explicitly.
- **Pull:** each location's *separate* Google/Yelp/Facebook listings — search per
  location, don't reuse one listing for all.

## Wellness / Boutique / Professional Services (yoga, studios, coaches, clinics)

- **Mood:** airy, calm, premium, personal for wellness; clean and credible for pro
  services. Built around trust in a person.
- **Primary CTA:** "Book a Session" / "Get Started" / "Request a Consult."
- **Signature sections:** hero with the practitioner's promise; About-the-person story;
  offerings/packages with real pricing; how-it-works; testimonials; booking/contact.
- **Safety:** no health/outcome guarantees; credentials only if verified.
