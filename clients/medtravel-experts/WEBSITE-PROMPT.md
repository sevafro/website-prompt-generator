## 1. Project Summary

Build a bilingual (Russian / English) marketing website for **MedTravel Experts**, an international medical travel company serving Russian-speaking and English-speaking clients living in the US and Canada. The company organizes medical trips abroad — check-ups, diagnostics, treatment, surgery, rehabilitation, and wellness/health-resort programs — by matching clients with accredited clinics worldwide.

**Core message / hero tagline:**
> "International healthcare — fully organized. Check-ups, treatment, wellness and rehabilitation worldwide."

**Positioning statement (use in About / Hero):**
> "We find the right doctor and clinic anywhere in the world — matched to your situation, budget and goals. We are not a clinic. We are not a travel agency. We are your personal navigator in international healthcare."

**Tone:** Warm, trustworthy, calm, expert — never salesy or clinical-cold. Think "a knowledgeable friend who handles the logistics so you don't have to." Reassuring for people making health decisions for themselves or aging parents.

## 2. Brand Assets

- **Logo:** provided (MedTravel Experts logo — plane icon + wordmark). Use as-is; do not redesign.
- **Color palette** (pulled from the logo):

Avoid stock-photo clichés (forced smiles, generic handshake shots). Include destination flag/country visuals where relevant (Turkey, South Korea, Germany, Spain, UAE, Thailand, Mexico, Czech Republic, Switzerland, Austria, etc.).

### 5.3 Medical Tourism (Service 1) 🏥
- **Intro:** We organize comprehensive check-ups, diagnostics, treatment, surgical procedures, specialist consultations, and second medical opinions at accredited international clinics. We don't just help clients choose a country or clinic — we help them identify the right medical solution based on diagnosis, travel goals, budget, timeline and personal preferences.
- **What's included** (bullet grid):
  - Comprehensive check-ups for women, men, children and seniors
  - Preventive screenings and early diagnostics
  - Specialized diagnostic programs
  - Consultations with international specialists and online pre-trip consultations
  - Second medical opinion
  - Review of diagnosis and proposed treatment plan
  - Access to alternative treatment options
  - Planned treatment and surgical procedures
  - Organization of care for complex and rare conditions
  - Dental treatment
  - Aesthetic medicine
  - Post-treatment follow-up and monitoring
- **Second Medical Opinion subsection** — when it matters:
  - Complex/unclear diagnosis, recommended serious surgery, long-term/costly treatment proposed, current treatment not working, patient wants alternative approaches, needs a highly specialized international expert's conclusion
  - What the consulting physician can do: confirm/clarify diagnosis, recommend additional tests, review treatment plan, outline alternatives, indicate if an in-person visit is required, provide a preliminary assessment and action plan
- **Key medical specialties** (icon/tag grid): Plastic & aesthetic surgery, Hair transplantation, Dentistry & implantology, Bariatric surgery & obesity treatment, Ophthalmology, Reproductive medicine & IVF, Oncology, Cardiology & cardiac surgery, Orthopedics & joint replacement, Neurosurgery & neurology, Gynecology & urology, Gastroenterology & endocrinology, Pediatrics, Dermatology, Diagnosis of rare/complex conditions
- **Clinic selection criteria** — clinics must: hold a valid national medical license; be authorized to treat international patients; have an international patient department/coordinator; provide an official medical proposal with scope and pricing; follow secure medical-records handling; disclose physician qualifications; have a clear post-treatment follow-up system
- **Accreditations we consider:** JCI (Joint Commission International), GHA (Global Healthcare Accreditation), Temos International, ISO quality standards, KAHF (Korean Accreditation for Hospitals Serving Foreign Patients), national licenses/accreditations
- **Disclaimer:** Accreditation is one of the selection criteria and does not guarantee treatment outcomes. The qualifications of the specific physician are assessed separately.
- Link out to relevant destinations (see §5.6)

### 5.4 Preventive Health & Wellness (Service 2) 🌿
- **Intro:** Not only for people already diagnosed — this service helps individuals proactively assess health, identify risk factors, restore physical resources and combine a health-focused program with a comfortable trip abroad. *"Taking care of your health before a serious problem arises."* Medical wellness combines diagnostics, prevention, recovery, nutrition, physical activity, natural therapeutic factors and comfortable travel — unlike a standard spa, it starts with a physician assessment and personalized health plan.
- **Who it's for:**
  - Haven't had a comprehensive check-up in a long time; want to catch risks before symptoms appear; chronic fatigue/stress/sleep disorders; reduced energy and performance; weight/metabolic health concerns; prevention of age-related conditions; women in perimenopause/menopause; men interested in age-related prevention; recovery after illness/surgery; anyone combining travel with meaningful health care
- **Program types** (accordion or card grid, each with sub-bullets):
  - **Comprehensive Diagnostics & Early Screening** — general check-up; cancer & cardiovascular screening; stroke risk & metabolic syndrome assessment; women's/men's health programs; programs for ages 50+ and 60+; pediatric preventive check-ups
  - **Longevity & Healthy Aging** — biological age & risk assessment; body composition & fitness analysis; sleep & stress evaluation; personalized nutrition planning; cognitive health programs; long-term health plan development
  - **Metabolic Health & Weight Management** — lab diagnostics & metabolic assessment; endocrinologist consultation; type 2 diabetes prevention; medically supervised weight loss programs
  - **Gut Health & Mayr Programs** — digestive system diagnostics; individualized therapeutic nutrition; microbiome support programs
  - **Sleep & Stress Recovery** — sleep quality assessment; nervous system recovery programs; physiotherapy, massage, digital detox; burnout prevention programs
  - **Thermal & Balneological Programs** — mineral baths, mud therapy, thermal pools; mineral water drinking courses; climate therapy, inhalations, therapeutic exercise
- **Typical program duration:** Introductory/diagnostic 3–5 days · Standard wellness 7–14 days · Comprehensive recovery 14–21 days
- Link out to relevant destinations (see §5.6)

### 5.5 Rehabilitation (Service 3) 💆
- **Intro:** We organize individualized recovery programs following surgery, injury, stroke, serious illness or extended treatment. The key advantage of specialized rehabilitation centers is intensive multidisciplinary teamwork and the ability to receive several therapy types within one coordinated program.
- **When rehab may be needed:** after stroke/neurosurgery; after joint replacement; after fractures/complex trauma; after spinal surgery; after oncological treatment; after cardiovascular surgery; chronic neurological conditions; impaired movement/speech/independence; children with motor and developmental disorders
- **Rehabilitation specialties** (card grid, each with sub-bullets):
  - **Neurological** — post-stroke recovery; traumatic brain/spinal cord injuries; Parkinson's, MS; balance/coordination disorders; speech, swallowing & cognitive recovery
  - **Orthopedic** — recovery after joint replacement; spinal surgery, fractures, sports injuries; mobility & muscle strength restoration; chronic pain management
  - **Cardiac** — recovery after heart attack/cardiac surgery; supervised physical activity programs; respiratory therapy & risk factor management
  - **Pulmonary** — recovery after serious lung conditions; breathing exercises & endurance training; management of chronic breathlessness
  - **Oncological** — recovery after surgery/chemo/radiation; nutritional support & physiotherapy; gradual return to daily activities
  - **Pediatric** — cerebral palsy & motor development disorders; physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy; parent training for home continuation
- **Rehabilitation methods** (bullet grid): individual physiotherapy & therapeutic exercise; mechanotherapy & robotic rehabilitation; hydrotherapy; occupational & speech therapy; cognitive rehabilitation; manual therapy & massage; laser & shockwave therapy; electrical stimulation; nutritional support; psychological support; caregiver/family training
- **Disclaimer:** PRP, ozone therapy and IV infusions are applied only after physician assessment and when medically indicated.
- **Combined programs callout:** With physician agreement, multiple goals can be addressed in a single trip — check-up + preventive health, diagnostics + second opinion, surgery + post-op rehab, dental treatment + recovery stay, rehabilitation + health resort, medical wellness + preventive screening. *"One trip — a comprehensive approach to your health."*

## 7. Design Direction

- Clean, modern, editorial-medical aesthetic — closer to a premium concierge health brand than a generic clinic template. Think calm confidence, not clinical sterility.
- Generous white space, soft rounded cards, subtle shadows.
- Use the brand blue for primary actions/links, red sparingly as an accent (badges, small highlights) — don't let red dominate, it can read alarming in a healthcare context.
- Icons for the 3 services (🏥🌿💆 concept, replace emoji with custom line icons in production) and for each medical specialty/program.
- Country cards on Destinations should use flags + clean typographic hierarchy.
- Mobile-first: stack service cards vertically, collapsible accordions for long specialty/program lists on mobile.
